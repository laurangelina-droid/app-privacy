Privacy Policy

This policy covers the iPhone games Seahorse Reef and The Lonely Highway, the money diary app Coinchi (iPhone, iPad and web), the bill splitter Patungan: Split Bill Calc, and the baby feeding tracker Nestling. Last updated: 23 October 2026.

We collect nothing

None of these apps collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your phone

To remember your progress, the games save a few things on your device only: your best score, the distance you have driven, your happiness level, the items you collected, the in-game friends you made and your sound settings. This information never leaves your phone. Deleting the app deletes it.

Coinchi and your money entries

Coinchi saves the amounts, categories, notes and dates you type in, plus your pet, pet name, currency and sound settings, only on your device (on the web version, only in your browser). Nothing is sent to us or anyone else, and Coinchi never connects to a bank. You can erase everything at any time with "Delete all data" in the My Pet tab, or by deleting the app.

Patungan, the camera and your photos

Patungan uses the camera, or a photo you pick from your gallery, only to read the prices on a receipt. The text is read on your phone with Apple's built-in text recognition. Photos are not saved by the app, not uploaded and not shared. The bill and names you type stay on your phone while you use the app; Patungan only remembers your chosen currency and the names of friends you added, so you can add them again quickly. When you choose to share a result, it goes only to the app you pick (for example WhatsApp), through the standard iPhone share menu.

Nestling and your baby's information

Nestling saves your baby's name, gender, birth date, weight, body length and feeds per day, and the breastfeeding times, pumping amounts and milk feeds you log, only on your iPhone. Nothing is sent to us or anyone else, and Nestling has no account, no internet connection, no ads and no analytics. Logs older than 3 years are removed automatically. You can erase everything at any time by deleting the app. The milk amounts Nestling shows are a general guide, not medical advice.

Internet

The apps work fully offline and do not connect to the internet.

Children

The apps are suitable for all ages and do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Contact and support

Questions or problems with any of these apps? Open an issue at github.com/laurangelina-droid/app-privacy/issues.
