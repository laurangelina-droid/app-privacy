Privacy Policy

This policy covers the iPhone games Seahorse Reef and The Lonely Highway, and the money diary app Coinchi (iPhone, iPad and web). Last updated: 17 October 2026.

We collect nothing

None of these apps collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your phone

To remember your progress, the games save a few things on your device only: your best score, the distance you have driven, your happiness level, the items you collected, the in-game friends you made and your sound settings. This information never leaves your phone. Deleting the app deletes it.

Coinchi and your money entries

Coinchi saves the amounts, categories, notes and dates you type in, plus your pet, pet name, currency and sound settings, only on your device (on the web version, only in your browser). Nothing is sent to us or anyone else, and Coinchi never connects to a bank. You can erase everything at any time with "Delete all data" in the My Pet tab, or by deleting the app.

Internet

The apps work fully offline and do not connect to the internet.

Children

The apps are suitable for all ages and do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Contact and support

Questions or problems with any of these apps? Open an issue at github.com/laurangelina-droid/app-privacy/issues.
