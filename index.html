Privacy Policy

This policy covers the iPhone games Seahorse Reef and The Lonely Highway. Last updated: 13 October 2026.

We collect nothing

The games do not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your phone

To remember your progress, the games save a few things on your device only: your best score, the distance you have driven, your happiness level, the items you collected, the in-game friends you made and your sound settings. This information never leaves your phone. Deleting the app deletes it.

Internet

The games work fully offline and do not connect to the internet.

Children

The games are suitable for all ages and do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Contact and support

Questions or problems with either game? Open an issue at github.com/laurangelina-droid/app-privacy/issues.
